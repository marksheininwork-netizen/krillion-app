## Draft product page copy

**Promotional text**

**Description**  
Put your knowledge to the test in Krillion, a quick-fire trivia game built for short breaks and friendly personal bests.

Play seven timed rounds across science, geography, maths, nature, history, space, language, art, technology, food, music, and sports. Pick the right answer before the countdown ends, then see your score and try again.

Features:
- Seven questions per game
- A fresh selection from a varied question pool
- A 20-second timer for each round
- Best score and games played saved on your device
- No account required
- Support, privacy, and terms pages included

Krillion is for entertainment. Scores stay on your device.
